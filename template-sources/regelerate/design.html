From building blocks to a network.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project

DescriptionDesignEngineeringResultsImplementationContribution

The proposed design combines self-assembling protein fibres with multimeric nodes. The nodes are intended to connect fibres into a three-dimensional network.

Specific binding architectures, sites and assembly timing remain design questions. The homepage animation illustrates possible arrangements rather than a validated molecular mechanism.
